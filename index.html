🧪 How to Test Near Your Car - Accurately

This is a defensive test. Do it only with your own car, in your own space.

  • 1. Baseline: Park, lock car, stand 2m away. Tap Start. Wait 60 seconds. Mark your phone, watch, earbuds as Trusted.
  • 2. Normal: You should see only your known devices. No alert.
  • 3. Anomaly test: Turn on a second phone's Bluetooth / a BLE beacon and leave it near the car for 2 minutes. Flipperguard should list it as Unknown.
  • 4. Alert test: If that unknown device stays close (RSSI stronger than -60 dBm) and is seen 3+ times, the alert appears. That is the intended behaviour.
  • 5. Stop: Always tap Stop when done. Do not leave scanning on unnecessarily.

Note: RSSI varies with distance, body, and obstacles. This tool is an early warning aid, not a forensic instrument. Pair with a Faraday pouch for key-fob protection.
